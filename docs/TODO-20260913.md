## 6. 🧪 The ML thread — the fourth predictor, and its boundary

Add a **workload-feature** model on this project's own multi-chip data, as the missing row beside
the constant (0.837%), the curve rule (0.675%) and probe-Ridge (0.883% — loses). Features are
computable: arithmetic intensity per workload (`gemm` ~1365 FLOP/byte, `membw` 0.167), plus

🛑 **The deliverable is WHEN per-workload prediction earns its measurement cost — not a win.** Do
not tune until it beats the constant; `predict_optimal_frequency.py` prints a verdict against
itself and that must not be "fixed". ⚠️ n = 12 workloads per chip; leave-one-out on 12 is weak and
every figure must say so.
