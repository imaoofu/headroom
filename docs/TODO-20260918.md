## 14. ✅ What the 6 mV step is — **answered 2026-09-18, as far as public documentation allows**

There is **no universal NVIDIA step**; NVIDIA's OpenVReg spec defines `Vstep = (Vmax − Vmin)/Nmax`.
Controllers differ (MP2884A command path 6.25 mV vs its own `READ_VOUT` at 1 mV; XDPE132G5C 5 or
10 mV; uP9512R 10 mV). **Measured here: 6.25 mV on the 2060 Super, 3060 and 3070 Ti; 5 mV on the
5060 Ti.** ⚠️ The grid is measured; **which controller is on these four boards is still unverified**,
and that is what remains open.
