## 7. Re-weight the contribution sentence

Its opening clause — *"we reshape the vendor's voltage-frequency curve region by region"* —

PenguinBurner. The load-bearing half is what follows: the optimum tracks the **floor region** and
not the region above it, with a negative control and registered predictions. Change the emphasis,
not the claim.

the section does not yet say so. Cheap, and it closes the likeliest attack on the largest body of
original work here.

## The standing protocol, so an unowned card is never wasted

Cards arrive unpredictably and leave fast. `docs/REGISTERED-PREDICTIONS.md` §4 now holds the
predictions in advance so a card that appears for a day can be **measured** the same day rather
than designed for. 🔑 That is the only way an unpredictable supply becomes evidence — and the
ownership question is settled: these are builds assembled to sell, owned outright on the bench, so
the safety invariant does not apply to them.
