## Review: what Claude checked beyond the acceptance scripts, and what changed

endings. The change is exactly the requested additions, and the docstring is accurate.
- ⛔ **It changed the default it was told to keep.** It skipped a slot with an empty `VFCurve` in
  **both** modes. Previously the default raised an error, as the spec required it to keep doing.
  Fixed: the skip applies only with `allow_partial`.
- The final newline was also restored.
- The existing 25 checks in `analysis/test_decode_profiles.py` still pass. **Seven new checks pin the
  new behaviour:**
  - the default still refuses the 3-slot store;
  - the tail check stays on unless allowed;
  - all three 3070 Ti slots reproduce the hand-decoded table;
  - next-record pairing reads the 5060 Ti's rung B 845 mV point as **2362 MHz, the curve editor's
    value**, not the stored sum of 2538.

  That last check is independent corroboration: the model never saw it, and it agrees with evidence
  from the other card.

**L2 (fine-pair comparison): accepted with one fix; attempt 2 used.** Read in full. It follows the
spec exactly and handles the no-clean-points case.
- ⛔ **`target_frequency_mhz` was not in its required columns**, so a file lacking it failed with a
  bare `KeyError`. Fixed.
- `analysis/test_compare_fine_pair.py` was added (3 tests):
  - a committed sweep against itself gives zero at every point;
  - hand-computed arithmetic, including a dirty point excluded from the statistics;
  - the three refusals, plus exit 2.

  computed independently on 09-23: −1.09 to −1.27% per point, argmax 1500 and 1455.

**L3 (inventory): the facts are accepted, the classification is not.** Attempt 2 is attached verbatim:

- **§5.4.2 has nothing to pin.** Its one "number" is the label in "`gemm` pass 1". ROADMAP had
