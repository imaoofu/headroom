## MP2884A: direct datasheet check completed

I opened the manufacturer's [MP2884A Rev. 1.02 datasheet](https://www.monolithicpower.com/en/documentview/productdocument/index/version/2/document_type/Datasheet/lang/en/sku/MP2884AGU/) (108 pages, dated 2025-11-14). Printed page 7, under “DAC (Reference Voltage),” specifies **6.25 mV** resolution/LSB. Printed pages 39–40 specify **6.25 mV/LSB** for `VOUT_COMMAND` (21h), a PMBus VID override command, and for `VOUT_OFFSET` (23h). Printed page 68 says `READ_VOUT` (8Bh) records the **sensed output voltage** and specifies **1 mV/LSB**. The document also identifies VOSEN/VORTN as differential remote sense pins at the load (printed page 4) and describes the load voltage sense amplifier (printed page 14).

**Verdict:** the two numbers, register roles, and “sensed output voltage” wording in the [earlier voltage-grid record](2026-09-18-nvidia-voltage-quantisation.md) are confirmed. This is a register's reporting step, not a claim of 1 mV absolute accuracy, nor evidence that any tested board has this controller or that HWiNFO reads its `READ_VOUT` register. The older record's “not independently opened here” row remains an accurate statement about that earlier session; this check supersedes its unresolved status.

## Reddit undervolting recipe: source opened, method number corrected

I opened the original August 2022 [“Method 4” Reddit post](https://www.reddit.com/r/nvidia/comments/wghb9d/i_present_to_you_method_4_of_undervolting_your_gpu/), including its method definitions and editing steps. The poster's **Method 3** keeps the points below the undervolt target at stock speeds and flattens the points above it. **Method 4** keeps the lowest idle points at stock and gradually raises the intervening points before flattening the upper curve. Thus the queued statement “Method 4 ... retaining stock curve points below the target” conflates the two methods. The post itself documents both shapes.

The project’s Profile 2 repair has zero offset throughout its lower region and a flat upper plateau, according to [the profile record](../AFTERBURNER-PROFILES.md). The *shape* of stock lower points plus a flattened upper region was public by August 2022. The Reddit post addresses stability and effective core clock, not an XBAR-linked bandwidth loss, a controlled repair comparison, or an advance bandwidth prediction. This sharpens, but does not erase, the narrower contribution boundary in the [earlier XBAR prior-art record](2026-09-18-xbar-prior-art.md).

## Related unresolved Figure 4 discrepancy

I opened [Wang et al.'s arXiv paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.00486), Figure 4 and §§5.1.1–5.2. The authors say their Figure 4 uses **20 selected benchmarks with good fits**, based on **5 voltage/core-frequency samples × 4 memory-frequency samples** per application; the released table previously audited in this repository has **30 applications**. Their reported optimum is derived from a fitted, continuously adjustable model, and their 36.4% wide-window case is simulated. These are real differences from a discrete raw-table argmax. The paper does not give a mapping from its 20 benchmark indices to the 30 released CSV application names in the passages examined. Therefore the repo's **18/30 highest-grid-point** result and their “close to the allowed lowest setting” statement are **not reconciled** by the available text. Do not cite one as a contradiction of the other without that mapping and matched energy definition.
