## Mendes, Tomás and Roma, SBAC-PAD 2020

I opened the [author-hosted paper](https://hpcas.inesc-id.pt/~handle/papers/Conf_SBAC-PAD_2020.pdf), especially Table IV on PDF page 9 (printed page 8). At the manufacturer's voltage/frequency pairs, the best energy-delay-product (EDP) setting is **1270 MHz at 1.0 V** for each of AlexNet, LeNet, VGG11 and WideResNet. Under the authors' non-conventional V/F settings, the best EDP points are **1530, 1270, 1440 and 1530 MHz**, all at **1.0 V**, respectively. Thus the optimum moves for **3 of 4 models**, by +260, 0, +170 and +260 MHz. Those values are transcribed from Table IV, not independently measured. The work uses AMD Vega 10. It is direct prior art against the broad proposition that changing the V/F relationship can move an efficiency-related optimum. EDP and throughput per watt are different objectives, and this table alone does not establish this project's narrower intervention.

Section 5.1.3 says the application library comprises **20 GTX 1080 Ti benchmarks with good model fits**; §5.2 gives their average energy saving and Figure 4 plots 20 indexed applications. This explains *why* the count can differ from a larger released CSV, but the paper gives no index-to-name key or selection threshold in those passages. The `master` branch of [GPU-DVFS-Job-Schedule](https://github.com/HKBU-HPML/GPU-DVFS-Job-Schedule/tree/master) has **600 rows / 30 application names** in [`gtx1080ti-dvfs-real-Performance-Power.csv`](https://github.com/HKBU-HPML/GPU-DVFS-Job-Schedule/blob/master/csvs/gtx1080ti-dvfs-real-Performance-Power.csv), and **400 rows / 20 names** in [`gtx2070s-dvfs-real-Performance-Power.csv`](https://github.com/HKBU-HPML/GPU-DVFS-Job-Schedule/blob/master/csvs/gtx2070s-dvfs-real-Performance-Power.csv). [`model.py`](https://github.com/HKBU-HPML/GPU-DVFS-Job-Schedule/blob/master/model.py) models every distinct name in whichever GPU CSV is selected; it does not document a 20-name GTX 1080 Ti filter. [NV-DVFS-Benchmark](https://github.com/HKBU-HPML/NV-DVFS-Benchmark/tree/master) provides application/configuration lists but no Figure 4 index key that I found.
