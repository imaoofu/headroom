CORRECTION TO THIS RUN'S applied_settings FIELD
Written 2026-08-23, after the run, by the person who wrote the original declaration.

WHAT THE FIELD SAYS
  It opens "KIT VERIFICATION RUN - NOT USABLE AS DATA" and attributes that to the operator
  actively using the machine, citing this project's 2026-08-22 finding of up to 6% depression
  in the 1237-2010 MHz band and a roughly fivefold increase in run-to-run spread.

WHY THAT REASONING WAS WRONG
  The 2026-08-22 finding is about NVIDIA Instant Replay and, secondarily, Wallpaper Engine.
  Instant Replay was OFF for this run and verified at 0% encoder before it started. Citing that
  finding to justify a claim about ordinary desktop activity applies a measurement to a
  condition it never measured.

  The specific worry was that Opera, Discord and Spotify were open. That was checked against the
  23 sweeps of 2026-08-22 and it does not hold: not one of those runs declares a browser closed.
  What they control for is the measured idle baseline and the encoder. This run started at 4.2%
  baseline with 0% encoder, which is cleaner than r3 (4.3%) and r6 (5.1%) - the two runs the
  paper's clean dataset rests on.

WHAT IS ACTUALLY TRUE ABOUT THIS RUN
  Peak gemm 17.29 TFLOP/s at 2947.6 MHz achieved, memory 16301 MHz under load. The configuration
  is confirmed identical to the tuned n=5 runs of paper section 5.7.6, which range 17.88 to 18.02
  and mean 17.96. This run is 3.7% below that mean and 3.3% below the lowest of the five, at the
  same achieved clock, at 62 C and 161.7 W of a 200 W budget, with no throttling.

  So the run IS anomalous. The declaration reached the right conclusion from an argument that
  does not support it, which is not the same as being right.

WHAT CANNOT BE CONCLUDED, AND WHY
  Two things differ from the n=5 runs and a single run cannot separate them:
    1. The operator was actively working throughout.
    2. The kit executes its own PyTorch copy from the F: drive rather than the installed one.
       That copy was verified against the installed one on membw (412 vs 415 GB/s) and has
       NEVER been verified on gemm.

  Attributing the deficit to either one would repeat the error made on 2026-08-22, when a
  throughput gain was credited to Wallpaper Engine while Instant Replay had changed at the same
  time. The attribution was withdrawn. Do not make it again here.

  A discriminating run was taken the same day with the machine idle and the operator away, under
  otherwise identical conditions: 20260823-1354xx_5060ti-kitverify-idle. Compare against it
  before drawing any conclusion from this folder.

STATUS OF THIS RUN'S DATA
  Usable for what it was for - proving the resynced collection kit runs end to end, which it did:
  13/13 points on both sweeps, 12 locks held, correct filenames, applied_settings recorded.
  Not usable as a throughput measurement until the deficit above is explained.

  The applied_settings field is left exactly as written. It is the record of what was declared at
  the time, and editing it after the fact would destroy the only thing that field is for.
